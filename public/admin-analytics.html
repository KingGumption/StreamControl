              How to read this report

              Meaningful interactions: quiz joins and accepted answers, song requests and commands, Hill votes, and completed Polaroids. Lifecycle events and answer results appear in the ledger but do not add to this total. Platform mix and the timeline use this same definition.

              People and repeat play: accounts use platform IDs where available, falling back to names. The same person on Twitch and YouTube counts as two accounts. Repeat quiz players joined at least two distinct games within this period; this is repeat participation, not proof of long-term retention.

              Time and comparisons: ranges are rolling windows ending when the report loads. Comparisons use the immediately preceding equal-length window. A previous value of zero is shown as New rather than a misleading percentage increase. Timeline buckets use UTC; table timestamps use your browser's local timezone.

              Game outcomes: completion rate is the proportion of games started in the period that also completed in the period. Completed totals may include games started earlier. A last quiz survivor keeps the crown while continuing their run; their final incorrect or missed answer ends that run without counting as an elimination. Defeat means everyone was eliminated together.

              Viewer measurements: engagement is the percentage of observed chatters who used a tool, not all viewers. Viewer averages use available samples; viewer hours are estimates. Combined peak viewers sum each platform's peak, which may occur at different times. End / peak is a viewer-count ratio, not individual viewer retention. Tool comparisons show correlation, not causation.

              Coverage and limits: preview quiz games do not record analytics or wins. Explicitly marked test events and test Polaroids are excluded. Missing telemetry is shown as Collecting rather than invented data. Summary totals use all matching records; tables show the latest 250 activity events, 30 streams / games, and top 25 participants. The downloaded JSON contains the displayed report and these same limits. Automatic refresh runs every 30 seconds.
