                How to read this report

                Meaningful interactions: quiz joins and accepted answers, song requests and commands, Hill votes, accepted arcade votes and guesses, and completed Polaroids. Lifecycle events and answer results appear in the ledger but do not add to this total. Platform mix and the timeline use this same definition.

                People and repeat play: accounts use platform IDs where available, falling back to names. The same person on Twitch and YouTube counts as two accounts. Repeat quiz players joined at least two distinct games within this period; this is repeat participation, not proof of long-term retention.

                Time and comparisons: ranges are rolling windows ending when the report loads. Comparisons use the immediately preceding equal-length window. A previous value of zero is shown as New rather than a misleading percentage increase. Timeline buckets use UTC; table timestamps use your browser's local timezone.

                Game outcomes: completion rate is the proportion of games started in the period that also completed in the period. Completed totals may include games started earlier. A last quiz survivor keeps the crown while continuing their run; their final incorrect or missed answer ends that run without counting as an elimination. Defeat means everyone was eliminated together.

                Viewer measurements: engagement is the percentage of observed chatters who used a tool, not all viewers. The overview mean gives each measured session equal weight. Within sessions, viewer averages are weighted by available sample counts within each platform. Viewer hours integrate only pairs of samples at most five minutes apart; unobserved time is excluded and a lone sample cannot establish watch time. Aligned minute peaks sum platform minute means only where every listed platform was measured in the same minute; they are estimated concurrent audience, not exact instantaneous peaks or unique people. Legacy peakViewers in exports still sums independent platform peaks for compatibility. Comparisons start at the observed beginning, which may be clipped by the date filter. Last sample / peak is a viewer-count ratio, not individual viewer retention. Tool comparisons show correlation, not causation.

                Coverage and limits: preview quiz games do not record analytics or wins. Explicitly marked test events and test Polaroids are excluded. Missing telemetry is shown as Collecting rather than invented data. Summary totals use all matching records. All stream roundups are shown and searchable; the activity ledger searches the whole period in pages of 100. Arcade history shows the latest 50 games. Quiz history shows the latest 30 games, Hill results the latest 40 rounds, song and failure ledgers the latest 30 rows, and participant rankings the top 25. The downloaded JSON contains the displayed report, including the current filtered activity page and these stated table limits. Automatic refresh runs every 30 seconds.
