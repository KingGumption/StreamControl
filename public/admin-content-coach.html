This release produces transparent statistical comparisons and rule-based experiment suggestions. It does not use an AI model to watch your videos or scrape other creators’ posts.

How comparisons work

  • Short-form and edited long-form are separate. Live streams and full stream replays are excluded from this workflow.
  • 24-hour snapshots allow 21–27 hours, 7-day snapshots 156–180 hours, and 28-day snapshots 648–696 hours after publication. These tolerances are approximate, not exact age matching.
  • Baselines require five earlier organic posts with a measured value, in the same platform, format, duration band and fixed observation window. Lifetime, paid and unknown-traffic observations are not benchmarked.
  • Short duration bands: up to 30s, 31–60s, over 60s. Long: up to 10m, over 10–30m, over 30m.
  • Use platform-native views consistently. Do not substitute YouTube engaged views for views. Platform view definitions differ; historical definition changes may also affect comparisons.
  • Average watched = average view duration ÷ content duration. It can exceed 100% through rewatching. It is not completion rate. Shares, saves and follows per 1,000 use views as the denominator.
  • Suggestions flag selected metrics below 80% of your median; posts reaching 1.5× median views prompt a follow-up idea. These thresholds are heuristics, not significance tests.
  • Missing data stays unavailable. No universal “viral score”, causal diagnosis or guaranteed reach is calculated.
  • Backups contain your raw observations, references and experiments. CSV exports can be reimported; JSON backups are for safekeeping.
